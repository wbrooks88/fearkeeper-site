Support

Something not working, or a question about how the app handles a rule? Here are answers to the questions that come up most, and how to reach us when they don't cover it.

Get in touch

The best way to report a problem or ask a question is to open an issue on the Fearkeeper support board (a free GitHub account is needed to post). It helps a lot to say which device you are using, which version of the app you have (shown at the bottom of Settings), and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot is always welcome.

Ask a question

Common questions

What do I get for free, and what does Pro add?

The free app covers a full campaign at the table: Fear, the spotlight, adversaries, environments, countdowns, the party, the Chronicle, a session plan, the dice tray, rules search, and basic recaps. Pro is a single one-time purchase that adds unlimited campaigns, encounters, scenes, and homebrew, one-tap adversary attacks, every countdown type, every backdrop theme, iCloud sync, importing and exporting, hosting Player mode for your players' phones, full recaps, and the Arcane features that use Apple Intelligence.

I bought Pro, but the app doesn't show it.

Open Settings in Fearkeeper and tap Restore purchases, making sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. If it still doesn't appear, check your internet connection and try again a few minutes later, since the App Store sometimes takes a moment to confirm.

How do I keep my campaigns on more than one device?

With Pro, turn on iCloud sync in Settings on each device, signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive switched on. Your games will appear on all of them. Without Pro, you can still keep everything safe with your device's normal backup.

My players can't open the Player mode page.

Their phones need to be on the same Wi-Fi network as your device, or joined to your own phone's hotspot. Some public, hotel, and school networks keep devices from seeing each other, and a hotspot is the easiest fix there. Also check that Fearkeeper is allowed to use the local network in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security.

Why can't I see Arcane Sounds or Arcane Recap?

These features use Apple Intelligence, so they only appear on devices that support it, with Apple Intelligence switched on in your device's Settings and Pro unlocked. Everything they hear stays on your device. The privacy policy explains exactly how.

Can I undo a mistake at the table?

Yes. Fearkeeper records every change as it happens, so Undo steps back through Fear, damage, the spotlight, and everything else, one change at a time.

Where do the adversaries and rules come from?

The adversaries, environments, and rules text come from the Daggerheart SRD 2.0, used under the Darrington Press Community Gaming License. Fearkeeper is an independent app and is not made or approved by Darrington Press or Critical Role. If you spot a rule the app gets wrong, please let us know.

Does Fearkeeper collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no ads, and your games stay on your device. See the privacy policy for the details.
