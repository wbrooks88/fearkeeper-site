Fearkeeper

A game master's companion for running Daggerheart in person, on iPad, iPhone, and Mac.

Fearkeeper sits beside you at the table and keeps track of the things that slip away mid-fight: your Fear, who holds the spotlight, the adversaries on the field and their hit points, the environment, countdowns, the party, and a running Chronicle of the session. You can plan scenes ahead of time, pull adversaries and environments straight from the SRD, and hand your players a page on their own phones so they can follow their characters.

Everything lives on your device. There are no accounts to make and nothing about you or your games is sent to us.
