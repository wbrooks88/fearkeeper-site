Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

The short version is that Fearkeeper does not collect anything about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads, and nothing about you or your games is ever sent to us.

What the app keeps, and where

Your campaigns, party, encounters, session plans, homebrew adversaries and environments, Chronicle, and recaps are saved on your device, inside the app's own storage. They stay there until you delete them or remove the app. We have no server that receives a copy, and we cannot see, read, or recover your data.

iCloud sync

If you own Fearkeeper Pro and choose to turn on iCloud sync, your games are also kept in your own iCloud Drive so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account can see them. That storage belongs to you and is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never see it. Turning sync off stops it, and you can manage or delete the files from your Apple Account's iCloud settings.

Microphone, Arcane Sounds, and Arcane Recap

On devices with Apple Intelligence, the optional Arcane Sounds and Arcane Recap features can listen to the table through the microphone, so the app can pick fitting music and write a recap of the session. They listen only while you have them switched on, and a LISTENING tag is shown the whole time. Speech is turned into text, and that text is read, by Apple's speech recognition and Apple Intelligence running on your device itself. Microphone audio is never recorded to a file, never uploaded, and never leaves your device.

So that a recap can be written or rewritten later, Arcane Recap keeps the words it heard as text in a private file on your device that is not included in backups. That text is deleted automatically after 30 days, and you can delete one session's heard words sooner from its recap. The app asks for microphone permission the first time you switch on a listening feature, and you can take that permission back at any time in your device's Settings.

Player mode

When you open your table to players, your device serves a small web page over your local Wi-Fi network (or your own phone's hotspot) so players can scan a code and follow their characters in a browser. That page and everything shown on it travel only across that local network, directly between your device and theirs. Nothing passes through the internet or through us, and the page stops when you close the table. Your device will ask for local network permission the first time you use this feature.

Sharing and exporting

If you export or share a campaign, encounter, character, or recap, the app hands the file to the place you pick, such as Files, AirDrop, Messages, or Mail. Where it goes from there is up to you and that service. The app never shares anything on its own.

Purchases

Fearkeeper Pro is a one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether Pro has been bought. We never receive your name, payment details, or Apple Account information.

Children

Fearkeeper does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins to handle data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

If you have a question about privacy, please ask on the Support page.
